Things it does

Small app. Surprisingly deep noodle.

Everything below ships in the box — no plugins to chase, no "pro tier" holding your features hostage.

Plain markdown, always

Type #, *, - [ ] and watch it render live. Your notes are just .md files with frontmatter — readable in 50 years, not locked in a database you'll never see.

the good stuff

Wiki-links that make your notes a brain

Wrap anything in [[double brackets]] and it becomes a link. Noodled tracks backlinks, spots broken ones, and can draw the whole tangle as a graph. Ideas stop being islands.

Local-first & private

Notes and attachments live in your database, on your WordPress, behind a passwordless PIN login. Files are served through a private gate — never a public URL — so nobody stumbles onto them.

Everywhere, in lockstep

Two-way GitHub sync keeps the desktop app and the web app identical. Edit on your laptop, pick up on your phone. Pulls quietly every 5 minutes.

Talk, don't type

Dictate straight into a note — tap the mic and your words land at the cursor. Or let Plaud drop transcribed recordings into a notebook while you're busy living.

for deep work

Zen, typewriter & reading modes

Fade everything but the line you're on. Keep your cursor centered. Or hit reading mode and let the chrome disappear. There's a Pomodoro timer and a word-count goal for the days you need a nudge.

CtrlP jump to note CtrlJ daily journal Ctrl. quick capture

Drop in a photo. Or fifty.

Hit + Photos and they land in a tidy gallery below your note — tap to open the lightbox, with the camera, date and location read from each shot's EXIF. Every image stays private to you.

for the whole house

A noodle for your people

Invite family with a one-tap email PIN. Share a notebook or a single note, read-only or read-write. Or give someone a drop folder — anything they put there shows up in your account under their name. Perfect for kids, parents, or a shared brain.

Connect the dots

Your thoughts were never a list.

Most apps file ideas into folders and forget them. Noodled lets them link to each other — so the mess in your head becomes a map you can actually walk.

The honest bit

It's your stuff. We mean it.

Look — plenty of note apps are lovely until the company gets bought, the prices climb, and your decade of thinking is hostage behind an export button that "isn't quite ready yet."

Noodled is the opposite bet. It's a self-contained plugin that lives on hosting you control. The notes are plain files. If we vanished tomorrow, you'd lose nothing but our company.

  • ✓ Plain .md on disk — open them in any editor, ever.
  • ✓ Bidirectional GitHub sync — you keep a full version history.
  • ✓ Passwordless PIN login, 1-year sessions, family sharing.
  • ✓ Installs as a PWA and works offline on the subway.
